## 🛡️ About Rurik Bruin Games

Based in Canada, **Rurik Bruin Games** is an independent workshop run by solo developer **Roderick Bernard Greening**, dedicated to crafting authentic Commodore 64 games and documenting the build process along the way.

### Why "Rurik Bruin Games"?

Every part of the studio's name and visual identity translates founder **Roderick Bernard Greening's** full name and initials into a retro computing banner:

* **Rurik (*Roderick*):** Drawn from the Old Norse *Hrœrekr* and East Slavic *Rurik*—the historical roots of *Roderick*, meaning *"famous ruler"* or *"rich in glory."*
* **Bruin (*Bernard*):** Inspired by the Old Germanic *Berinhard* ("brave as a bear") and the classic folkloric name for a brown bear—representing strength, endurance, and steady craftsmanship.
* **The R.B.G. & C64 Connection:** The initials **R.B.G.** bridge **R**oderick **B**ernard **G**reening with the **R**ed, **B**lue, and **G**reen pixels of classic computing—framed in an authentic 16-color Commodore 64 VIC-II palette honoring the 8-bit roots that inspired the studio.

### How We Build

We develop as natively as possible on real **Commodore 64 Ultimate** hardware and cycle-accurate emulation—writing code, building sprites, and composing SID audio directly inside the C64 environment. See the full hardware and software breakdown on our [Tools & Setup](/tools) page.
