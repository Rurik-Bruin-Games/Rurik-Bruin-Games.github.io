
**Why "Rurik Bruin Games"?**  
is an independent game studio rooted in heritage, craft, and bold storytelling. The name is a personal tribute to founder **Roderick Bernard Greening**, translating his full name and initials into a timeless banner:
 
-   **Rurik (_Roderick_):** Derived from the Old Norse _Hrœrekr_ and East Slavic _Rurik_, the direct historical root of the name Roderick—meaning _"famous ruler"_ or _"rich in glory."_
-   **Bruin (_Bernard_):** Drawn from the Old Germanic _Berinhard_ ("brave as a bear") and the classic folkloric name for a brown bear—representing strength, endurance, and steady craftsmanship.
-   **The Greening & RBG Connection:** The studio's visual identity honours the surname **Greening** (historically meaning _"dweller by the green"_ or _"new growth"_) and the initials **R.B.G.**—bridging the natural forest colours of the bear's world with the **R**ed, **B**lue, and **G**reen pixels at the heart of video game development.
