Welcome to the very first devlog for **Rurik Bruin Games**! 

First of all, who am I? Well, I am a programmer who worked solving other peoples problems for 30 years. And now, I am a retired programmer who wants to finally put those skills to use for fun and prosperity.

This site and log will document my journey, and ultimately, I hope, lead to some publishing of a finished product or two. As I work on those, I will be documenting my journey and publishing the odd article as a companion to my dev journey. 

So without further adieu, here is what I am working on this week:

* Writing an article on SID Wizard - My game will require some music and sound effects, so we need to learn how to make some tunes.
* Writing an article on Vision Basic - Vision Basic is my new best friend. This utility will be my main dev environment on the C64 creating lightning fast compiled BASIC.
* And finally, Building the studio website - Getting the site up and running is a critical step to allowing me to document everything and track my progress while sharing that journey woth anyone crazy enough to read my ramblings.

Everything here is still very much under construction. Its a huge work in progress. I havent had to manage a web site for a very long time and this will certainly take a bit of getting used to on my part. 

Onward and upward as they say...
